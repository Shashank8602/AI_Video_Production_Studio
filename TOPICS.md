## First video

**"If there are infinite stars, why is the night sky dark?"** (Olbers' paradox)

- Hook: Zib: "Why is the night sky dark if there are billions of stars?"
- Twist: the universe has a finite age, so light from far stars has not reached us; expansion also stretches the light.
- Wonder beat: we are looking back in time.
- Punchline: Bolt closes with a deadpan line.

Alternatives:
- "Would you feel it if you fell into a black hole?"
- "Why can't we see the edge of the universe?"
