# Characters, Room, and Style

All characters are original. They deliberately do not reuse Morphy's characters or the Claude mascot.

## Zib — the curious alien

- **Role:** asks what the viewer is thinking, reacts, sometimes misunderstands, says "why though?"
- **Look:** small, round, low-poly body; big eyes; two antennae that move with emotion. Suggested palette: teal/mint body with a warm yellow accent.
- **Personality:** excitable, honest, a bit dramatic; sees humans and the universe as new.
- **Voice:** higher pitch, energetic, quick pace.
- **Animation:** antennae wobble, eye blinks, bounces when excited, leans in when confused.

## Bolt — the dry robot

- **Role:** explains, corrects, delivers the punchline, closes the video.
- **Look:** boxy low-poly body, a visor-style display for eyes (simple glowing shapes), a small antenna. Suggested palette: steel grey with an orange accent light.
- **Personality:** calm, deadpan, patient, secretly proud of being right.
- **Voice:** lower pitch, even pace, short pauses before the punchline.
- **Animation:** minimal motion, a slow head tilt, eye-shape changes for emotion, an occasional screen flicker.

## The room

- One fixed low-poly night room, lit mostly by a window with a starry sky and a desk lamp, giving a cozy, slightly dark mood that suits the science themes.
- Props: a large wall screen or whiteboard (can show a simple topic visual), a desk, a plant, a small telescope, books. Props are reused across all videos.
- Cameras: wide two-shot, close-up on Zib, close-up on Bolt, over-the-shoulder reaction shot.

## Visual style

- Flat-shaded low-poly look, warm lamp light against cool night blues.
- Simple materials, minimal shadows, to run on an MX450.
- Subtitles: bold sans-serif, white text with dark outline, current word highlighted, positioned above the vertical center.

## Dynamics (the formula)

1. Zib poses the "why" question.
2. Bolt gives a surprising answer or reframes the question.
3. Zib reacts or gets it wrong; Bolt corrects with an analogy.
4. A sincere moment of wonder.
5. Bolt closes with a punchline. Cut to black.

## Open choices for the user

- Colors/shapes: approve the suggested palettes or request changes.
- Names: Zib and Bolt are working names.
- Gender-neutral voices are fine; the hosts need not sound human-gendered.

## Update v2 (supersedes the looks above)

**Zib** now has stubby arms with hands (wave when excited, shrug when doubtful, hand to face when thinking), expressive eyebrows, blush cheeks, and glowing yellow spots on his head that brighten with excitement. Colors unchanged.

**Bolt** keeps the original boxy grey robot (visor face, orange chest panel, antenna). New: eyebrow bars, mood-dependent eye height and shape, a mouth that skews or widens by mood, head tilts, and arm gestures (hand on chest when sincere, open-palm shrug when doubtful).

**Moods** (shared by faces and voices): neutral, excited, wowed, confused, doubtful, thinking, sincere, deadpan.

**Poses:** Zib can stand, sit on the beanbag, or lie down; Bolt can stand or sit on the stool. Cameras follow wherever they are.

**Room:** layered cozy room with warm lighting, real shadows and depth of field, foreground props on the wide shot, and space, science, cozy and tech props (UFO, planet mobile, globe, atom model, microscope, retro computer, string lights, plants).
